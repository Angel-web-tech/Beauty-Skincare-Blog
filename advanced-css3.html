About The Glow Edit

Beauty Begins With Care

The Glow Edit is a beauty and skincare blog created to make skincare simple and enjoyable.

Learn about skincare ingredients, daily routines, beauty essentials, and healthy skin habits.

Our goal is to help you glow naturally and feel confident in your own skin.

Skincare Glossary

Hydration

Adding water to the skin to keep it soft, fresh, and comfortable.

Exfoliation

Removing dead skin cells from the surface of the skin.

Antioxidants

Ingredients that help protect skin from environmental stress.

Moisturizer

A skincare product that helps maintain the skin's moisture barrier.

Sunscreen

A product that protects skin from harmful ultraviolet radiation.

Serum

A lightweight product containing concentrated skincare ingredients.
